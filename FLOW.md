# How This Works - The Simple Version

## The Only Rule: Dump and Ask

You capture things naturally wherever you are.
Claude organizes, tracks, and reminds you.

```
YOU (capture anywhere)              CLAUDE (does the work)
─────────────────────               ──────────────────────

Phone → Apple Notes                 Reads your raw notes
Laptop → OneNote                    Sorts into categories
Voice → just tell Claude            Updates trackers
Photo → screenshot a receipt        Extracts numbers
                                    Reminds you of things
        ┌─────────────┐            Spots patterns
        │  This Repo  │            Gives you weekly summaries
        │  (the brain)│
        └─────────────┘
```

## Your Daily Workflow

### Morning (30 seconds)
> "Claude, what's my day look like?"

Claude reads your trackers and tells you:
- Top priorities
- Upcoming deadlines
- Bills due soon
- Health streak status

### Throughout the Day
Just jot notes naturally in Apple Notes or OneNote. Don't organize them.
Write things like:
- "Lunch with team RM45"
- "Gym - bench press 60kg x 8"
- "Meeting with client - need to follow up on proposal by Friday"
- "Netflix renewed RM55"
- "Slept badly last night maybe 5 hours"
- "Interesting course on Coursera about AI"

### Evening or Whenever (60 seconds)
Paste your day's notes to Claude or just tell it what happened:
> "Here's what I did today: [paste notes or just talk]"

Claude will:
1. Log expenses to finance tracker
2. Log workouts/sleep to health tracker  
3. Add tasks to work tracker
4. Flag anything important

### Weekly (5 minutes, Sunday)
> "Claude, give me my weekly review"

Claude reads everything and gives you:
- Life scores
- Spending summary
- Health trends
- What went well / what needs attention
- Priorities for next week

## That's It.

You never need to manually edit YAML files.
You never need to organize notes.
You never need to update trackers yourself.

Just capture and ask Claude.
